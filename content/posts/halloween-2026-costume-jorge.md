For Halloween 2026, I kept the handmade costume tradition going after last year's [El Pibe Cabeza](/posts/paper-mache-halloween-pibe-cabeza/).

Meet **Jorge**!

The centerpiece of the costume is a giant, oversized handmade head: cardboard + paper mache (carta pesta) + acrylics.
It took me about a month to make it.

I watched a bunch of [how to make a paper mache head](https://www.youtube.com/@miquililintal/) from Miqui Lilintal YouTube channel.

You can watch the short video of Jorge in action here: [Jorge, my halloween costume for 2026 (YouTube Shorts)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VXb5MkKHJIk).

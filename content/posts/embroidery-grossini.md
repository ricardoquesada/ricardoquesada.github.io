Haven't you heard of Grossini? The one and only? The best ever magitian?

Shame on you! :-)

![grossini](/images/embroidery_grossini_full.jpg)

In 2006, with a group of friends (nubis, leito, lucio, tenuki, alecu, chaghi, pabloz, riq, facundobatista, yaco)
we created a video game called [Typus Pocus](https://pyweek.org/e/PyAr2/).

And the main character of the video game was Grossini, a sort-of failed magitian.

We had a lot of fun creating it, and created a few sequels were Grossini was, again, the main character.

This T-shirt is a homage to him, The Great Grossini.

This is the work-in-progress:
![grossini_wip](/images/embroidery_grossini_wip.jpg)

This is the back of the T-shirt, which I'm kind of proud of since it doesn't have too many "loops". This is because I'm using my new "algorithm" to create the paths and
there are almost no jump stitches:

![grossini](/images/embroidery_grossini_back.jpg)

After finishing the embroidery, when I manually remove the stabilizer, it is not uncomommon that I create a hole in the T-shirt with my scissors: the "bug".
To fix it, what I do is to embroider a square on top of the hole to hide it:

![grossini](/images/embroidery_grossini_extended.jpg)
